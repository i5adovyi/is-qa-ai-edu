# Lab 04 hints

<details><summary>Hint 1 — Validate Python types deliberately</summary>

`isinstance(True, int)` is true. If the contract excludes booleans, use an exact integer-type check or explicitly reject `bool`.

</details>

<details><summary>Hint 2 — Cap before constructing giant numbers</summary>

For an enormous attempt number, calculating `2 ** attempt` is unnecessary. Once the result must exceed the cap, return the cap.

</details>

<details><summary>Hint 3 — Use the cap's bit length</summary>

After handling zero base, a nonnegative integer base is at least one. When the attempt is at least `cap_ms.bit_length()`, the exponential is already at least the cap. Otherwise calculate the small exponent and apply `min`.

</details>
