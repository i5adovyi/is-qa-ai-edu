# Lab 08 hints

<details><summary>Hint 1 — Classify, then filter evidence</summary>

You may reuse the reference classifier. Filter supplied articles by matching topic before deciding whether evidence is sufficient.

</details>

<details><summary>Hint 2 — Abstain for a reason</summary>

Safety/unknown categories, no relevant evidence, and multiple relevant candidates should escalate. A known category alone is not enough to draft.

</details>

<details><summary>Hint 3 — Construct one of two exact shapes</summary>

Both actions use the same five keys. Escalation has empty reply/citations; drafting uses the selected article's answer and ID. Keep the reason short and operational.

</details>
